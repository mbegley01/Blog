**Introduction**

On this week's episodes of Love Island, the boys went on a vacation from the Villa to Casa Amor, where they were introduced to six new bombshells. Casa is supposed to be "the ultimate test of the couples' connections", as they have the freedom away from their couples for a few days to explore other connections. What really stuck out to me was the stark changes in some of the boys' behavior in the new environment. Some of the boys (KC cough cough) immediately showed their true colors when they thought they were not being watched. Others (Sincere and Corbin I am looking at you) had a more continuous streak of shady behavior. The first impression of the Casa girls was extremely sexual, and the guys were physical right away with them. KC was literally groping the girls' asses it was the most bizarre thing I have ever seen.

This was contrasted with the new male bombshells that came to stay at the Villa while the boys were away. Their first activity was a party where the Villa Girls could chat and get to know the new bombshells. Additionally, the new guys had a noticably different demeanor towards the women. They held themselves with more confidence and respect; there was a noticable difference in the maturity levels.

This got me thinking about how there are different ways to present masculinity. Masculinity describes the set of traits, behaviors, and roles socially associated with men. Although it has historically been associated with qualities like physical strength and independence, it also includes emotional intelligence and vulnerability in modern descriptions. Similar to personality, a person's masculinity is dependent on context and is variable over time, but each person has their own individual flavor. I developed a framework similar to 16 Personalities for categorizing archetypes for masculinity based on five axes. I will start by explaining each axis, then I will use the men of Love Island as examples.
