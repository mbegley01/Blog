Theorem: The number 67 is the funniest natural number in existence

Proof by contradiction: Suppose there exists a number X that is funnier than 67. 
Let $F(k)$ be a function $\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ given by:
$$
F(k) = 
\begin{cases} 
1000 & \text{if } k = 67\\
200 & \text{if } k = 69 \\
150 & \text{if } k = 21 \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

$F(X) < F(67) \forall X$, therefore there does not exist a number funnier than 67, contradicting our assumption. 
Thus there is no number funnier than 67, so 67 is the funniest number.
